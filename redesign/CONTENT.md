# VibeCloud: the audit (2026-10-26)

What the site was before the 2027 redesign, what of it works, and what it claims. The brief:
`docs/design/startup-redesign-2027.md` in CommandAGI, row VibeCloud.

## Routes

| route | what it is | kept |
|---|---|---|

| `/es/`, `/zh/` | the home page in Spanish and Simplified Chinese | yes, generated from the same layout |

## Working features (every one kept)

- **The console** (`app/console.js`, `plan.js`, `parse.js`, `catalog.js`): a sentence in English, Spanish or Chinese →
  the resources, the region, the ceiling → a plan on the sample catalog of five accounts (CommandAGI's placement
  rules), with "why not the others", an outage selector that moves a resource, and a simulated provision.
- **The language button** (A/文): a dialog with no script (invoker commands), focus on the current language, the
  remembered choice, the browser-language hint (`app/lang.js`).
- **Contact**: `mailto:hello@vibecloud.pro`.
- The CSP: no script or request to another host (`connect-src 'none'`).

## What is a demo

All of it. The console plans in the browser on a sample catalog with round prices; it calls no provider. The provider
accounts, the provisioners and the live failover runner are not built (the org's `docs/providers.md`).

## Claims, checked

- "plans, provisions, wires and runs it across AWS, Google Cloud and Cloudflare": the product's intent; the page now
  says on screen which parts are built and which are not (§05 of the home page).
- No customer, logo, user count, uptime or testimonial anywhere. The redesign adds none.
- Prices: "round numbers for a demo, not quotes" — kept, next to every price.

## What the redesign adds

- The ⌘K palette and the hero fleet (`app/fleet.js`, `app/topology.js`, `app/ops.js`): a command → a topology diff
  priced by the same planner → approve → apply → verified, all simulated and labeled so.
- The sample sentences as buttons (the `console.select` message existed with no control).
- Two generated loops (labeled generated), the wordmark, favicon, touch icon and OG image.
