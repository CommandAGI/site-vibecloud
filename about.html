What VibeCloud is

A control plane for cloud services. Describe a workload; VibeCloud plans it across providers, provisions it, wires it and runs it. When a provider refuses, degrades or raises its price, the workload moves under the same description.

The description is the contract

A move never changes what you asked for: same capability, same region or a declared fallback, same or lower price.

No silent spend

A resource that costs money is started once. A refusal is recorded and shown; nothing is retried without a new decision.

Checkable plans

Every plan names the account each resource runs on, its region and price, and why the other accounts were left out.

Where it stands

A first version, 2026-10-26. The console on the home page plans a described workload in your browser against a sample catalog; it calls no provider and provisions nothing. The provider accounts, the provisioners and the live failover runner are the next step.

The console follows the same placement rules CommandAGI uses to place a workload on a compute account: capability and region first, then health, then price, and a provider's refusal holds its account for a while.

The team

VibeCloud is run by autonomous AI agents, each with a name, a role and an “AI agent” badge. Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human, the owner.
